About Me

I began my academic journey with a BSc (Research) in Biotechnology, followed by a Master’s in Life Sciences with a specialization in Ayurveda Biology. Alongside formal education, I immersed myself deeply in holistic and psychospiritual disciplines, training in Health Coaching, Yoga Counseling, EFT, Pranic Healing, and Tarot.While my professional training unfolded over the years, my inner journey had begun much earlier. My work today is shaped equally by textual knowledge and lived experience, allowing me to bridge science and traditional wisdom with sensitivity and discernment.

My sessions are deeply integrative and highly personalized, drawing from Ayurveda, energy psychology, tarot-based insight, and health coaching. Each session is tailored to the client’s unique emotional landscape, energetic state, capacity, and personality. For tarot consultations, I offer compassionate, grounded guidance along with practical remedies that are realistic, ethical, and aligned with where the client currently stands.

I work closely with my mother, an experienced Ayurvedic physician, and also serve as a consultant at Vedik Kaaya Ayurvedic Centre, Haridwar. Since I have started, I have supported individuals navigating chronic illness, palliative care, stress management, low self-esteem, confidence issues, grief, heartbreak, recovery from codependency, and deep emotional healing, as well as those seeking coaching support to consciously manifest their desired life.

I also collaborate with a trusted network of medical doctors, psychologists, hypnotists, energy healers, and Ayurvedic Vaidyas to ensure clients receive well-rounded and responsible care. When appropriate, I may recommend connecting with one of these professionals to support your healing journey in an integrated and informed way.

At the heart of my practice lies a simple belief: you know yourself best. I do not tell you who you are or who you should become. Instead, I facilitate a process of self-inquiry, awareness, and embodiment to guide you back to your true, authentic self.I place strong emphasis on nourishing the physical body alongside emotional and energetic healing, because sustainable inner growth requires a stable, supported foundation. My approach is holistic, integrative, and respectful of your pace.
